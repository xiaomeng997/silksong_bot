
===== achievements (12 findings)
[ach0] (high,CRIT) The target achievements are Steel Soul (API key STEEL_SOUL, 'Finish the game in Steel Soul mode') and Speedrunner (SPEEDRUN_1, 'Complete the game in under 5 hours'). The related 100% variants are Speed Completion (SPEED_COMPLETION, '100% and finish in under 30 hours') and Steel Heart (STEEL_SOUL_FULL, '100% and finish in Steel Soul mode'). Completion is COMPLETION (100%).
[ach1] (high,CRIT) All four completion-type achievements are awarded in one function, GameManager.CheckCompletionAchievements(). It uses exact thresholds on playerData.playTime in seconds. Speedrunner does not depend on the mode, so one Steel Soul run can earn both STEEL_SOUL and SPEEDRUN_1 at once.
[ach2] (medium,CRIT) Any ending that rolls credits counts as 'finishing the game'. The fastest valid target is Ending 1, Weaver Queen (ENDING_A): beat Grand Mother Silk at the end of Act 2 without having done the Silk and Soul wish. Act 3 and the true ending are not required.
[ach3] (high,CRIT) The Speedrunner timer is the save file's in-game playTime, not real time. It only advances while GameState is PLAYING, ENTERING_LEVEL or EXITING_LEVEL, the inventory is closed, and the frame took less than 1 second.
[ach4] (high) Steel Soul is unlocked either by reaching the credits once or by entering the cheat code on the main-menu Extras page. No prior completion is needed.
[ach5] (high,CRIT) On a lethal death in Steel Soul the save is not deleted. Instead it is marked Dead, saved immediately, and the slot becomes 'DEFEATED'. There is no cocoon and no respawn, and the in-game restore-save button is hidden for that slot.
[ach6] (high,CRIT) The achievement check does not verify that the run was never restored. Restoring a backup, or editing permadeathMode from 2 back to 1, still passes the STEEL_SOUL check (permadeathMode > Off) and also rolls playTime back. This is the 'SL' method human Steel Soul + 5 h runners used.
[ach7] (high) Steel Soul mode changes some content but not core combat or physics. None of the changes are on the core Act 2 critical path, but the map and state model need Steel Soul variants.
[ach8] (high) There are no no-heal, no-bench, no-hit or pacifist style achievements. The only achievements that combine naturally with a Steel Soul speed run are the ending achievements (Weaver Queen / ENDING_A) and the progression achievements along the route. Completion, Steel Heart and Speed Completion need 100%, which is a much larger scope.
[ach9] (medium) No patch up to the latest PC version, 1.0.30000, changed the achievement thresholds, the timer logic or the Steel Soul death rule. The only achievement-related patch note was a wording change for the Herald's Wish achievement.
[ach10] (medium) The free Sea of Sorrow expansion was announced on 2025-12-16 for 'in 2026' and had not been released as of the latest sources found. The last official Steam announcement is patch 1.0.30000, called the 'last significant update' before the DLC.
[ach11] (medium) Human reference times suggest 5 h of in-game time is a loose limit for an optimized no-glitch route, so the bot's time budget can favour safety.
-- implications:
 * Single-run plan: a Steel Soul slot, ending at Weaver Queen (Grand Mother Silk at the end of Act 2, without starting the Silk and Soul wish). If the final saved playerData.playTime is 18000 s or less, the run earns both STEEL_SOUL and SPEEDRUN_1. Budget with a margin (for example 4.5 h, i.e. 16200 s), because the credits check reads playerData.playTime and it is unclear whether the session time is flushed to it before the check.
 * The time tracker should model the real timer rule: count PLAYING plus room transitions (ENTERING_LEVEL/EXITING_LEVEL); do not count the pause menu, inventory, CUTSCENE or menu loads. Read GameManager.PlayTime (sessionStartTime + sessionPlayTimer) from memory, or playTime from the decrypted user#.dat, rather than a wall clock. Room transitions do count, so a route with fewer transitions saves time.
 * The bot may use the pause menu or inventory as free thinking time for heavy planning, since neither advances the timer. Map reading through the inventory is also free. Whether the quick map pauses the timer is unverified.
 * The safety layer must act before the killing hit. Die() sets disablePause=true immediately, and the permadeath flag is saved during PlayerDead. An emergency 'pause, then Save & Quit' is only possible while pausing is allowed; since 1.0.29242, return-to-menu is also blocked whenever pause or save-quit is disabled. The trigger should fire when predicted incoming damage is at least current HP, including hazards (spikes or acid on the last mask are a real death).
 * Nonlethal zones (memory scenes such as Elegy of the Deep memories, and the First Sinner, Shakra, Garmond and Zaza spars) can be flagged as lower risk in the risk model.
 * For development and training, run the same route in Classic mode, or use slot backups (copy user#.dat and the Restore_Points folder), so failed attempts do not require restarting from zero. The achievement code does not detect restored or edited saves, and restoring also rolls playTime back. Whether the final run uses backups is the user's call: it works technically, but it goes against the permadeath intent.
 * Steel Soul can be unlocked with the Extras-menu code (Up, Down, Up, Down, Left, Right, Left, Right), so no prior full clear is needed. An automation script can enter it before selecting the Steel Soul new game.
 * Map and state database: include the Steel-Soul-only differences (dead Styx nest and the Skynx passage, the Steel Seer Zi room in Blasted Steps, Shell Satchel instead of Dead Bug's Purse, the Bone Bottom passage). Key world-state flags off the PlayerData fields (e.g. Unlocked*Station, permadeathMode, CompletedEndings) rather than pixels where possible.
 * Pin the game version: turn off Steam auto-update or use a depot download, and keep enemy and attack models per version. 1.0.28497 changed Moorwing, Sister Splinter and Sandcarver damage; 1.0.30000 changed Last Judge's explosion damage, added a Pale Lake bench and allowed Silk Soar through top exits. The latest PC version is 1.0.30000; the Sea of Sorrow expansion could change the world later.
 * The achievement code has no mod detection, so a BepInEx plugin that exports game state (HP, silk, position, enemy FSM states) should not block Steam achievements, based on the release-build code.

===== modding (21 findings)
[mod0] (high,CRIT) Silksong's Windows/Steam build uses the Mono scripting backend, not IL2CPP. Assembly-CSharp.dll is a normal managed .NET assembly that ILSpy/dnSpyEx decompile almost 1:1.
[mod1] (medium) The launch build runs on Unity 6000.0.50f1 (Unity 6). The engine was upgraded in a second build of patch 1.0.28714 (about October 2025), and I could not confirm the exact current Unity version.
[mod2] (medium,CRIT) The game version live on Steam as of the latest community sources is 1.0.30000 (24 March 2026). Every past version can be downloaded from Steam app 1030300, Windows depot 1030301, using the listed manifest IDs, so a bot can be pinned to one build.
[mod3] (high,CRIT) The standard loader is BepInEx 5.4.23.4 (x64). The community package is silksong_modding/BepInExPack_Silksong 1.0.3; BepInEx 6 is not used by the community.
[mod4] (high) The community's shared API is a set of BepInEx libraries from the 'silksong-modding' GitHub org and Thunderstore team. HK1's Modding API and Satchel were not ported; these libraries replace them.
[mod5] (high,CRIT) As in HK1, enemies and bosses are driven by PlayMaker FSMs. A plugin can read a boss's current state (for example Lace's attack) from PlayMakerFSM.ActiveStateName, its animation from tk2dSpriteAnimator.CurrentClip.name, and its HP from HealthManager.hp.
[mod6] (high,CRIT) DebugMod's HitboxRender contains a working classifier that sorts every Collider2D in a scene into enemy/hazard, terrain, player, player attack, transition gate, hazard-respawn, breakable, interactable and other. It can be reused directly as a ground-truth 'terrain plus threat' extractor.
[mod7] (high,CRIT) Player and world state is available as named Mono fields, both in-process and by reading memory externally. The key roots are GameManager._instance, its playerData field (a PlayerData object) and hero_ctrl.cState (HeroControllerStates).
[mod8] (high) The LiveSplit autosplitter is AlexKnauth/silksong-autosplit-wasm. It is written in Rust, compiled to WASM on LiveSplit's Auto Splitting Runtime (asr crate, Mono V3), and needs LiveSplit 1.8.34 or later. Official speedrun timing is LiveSplit load-removed time; the in-game timer is not accepted.
[mod9] (high) DebugMod (hk-speedrunning/Silksong.DebugMod, v1.1.2, MIT) is the main practice and debug tool. Its complete savestates, invincibility, noclip, timescale with frame advance, and hitbox viewer make it the natural training harness. Other mods can call its savestate API.
[mod10] (high,CRIT) Silksong.Benchwarp's data files hold a nearly complete, machine-readable world graph for navigation without buying maps. It includes transition obstacles tied to save-data keys, which model world-state changes such as shortcuts, one-way walls and Act 3 blocks.
[mod11] (high) The Archipelago randomizer's logic repository contains a room graph with in-room nodes, transitions to in-game gate IDs, and ability requirements in DNF form, including Act 3 state. It maps directly onto a planner for reachability and movement tech.
[mod12] (high) ItemChanger.Silksong (homothetyhk, a work-in-progress port of HK1 ItemChanger, LGPL) and jakobhellermann/silksong-data-dump give item and check locations with scene names and coordinates, shop costs, and game data tables.
[mod13] (medium,CRIT) Input runs through InControl, compiled into Assembly-CSharp (HeroActions/PlayerActionSet, InputHandler.inputActions). Since Patch 4, the device layer uses Unity's Input System. A proven in-process injection method is a Harmony prefix on UnityEngine.InputSystem.Controls.ButtonControl.get_isPressed.
[mod14] (medium,CRIT) I found no mod detection or save flagging. Achievements go through AchievementHandler to the platform layer (Steamworks.NET), and BepInEx itself does not block them. Community mods that block or unlock achievements exist, which implies mods alone do not disable them.
[mod15] (medium) The only DRM is Steam's relaunch-if-not-started-by-Steam check (SteamOnlineSubsystem.IsPackaged). There is no anti-cheat or code-integrity check, and the game is also sold on GOG (DRM-free) and Xbox PC.
[mod16] (medium) Saves are userN.dat files under %USERPROFILE%/AppData/LocalLow/Team Cherry/Hollow Knight Silksong/<SteamID>/, stored as a header plus Base64 plus AES-ECB-encrypted JSON (the same scheme as HK1). permadeathMode is 0 = normal, 1 = Steel Soul alive, 2 = Steel Soul dead.
[mod17] (medium) libTAS (Linux only) has worked with Silksong since September 2025, after fixes by Kilaye. The TASer Jarlyk published an Any% NMG TAS (52:59, TASVideos forum). I found no Windows TAS or input-replay mod for Silksong, and HK1's TasInfo tool was not ported.
[mod18] (high) Two Silksong RL/bot projects already exist. SilksongRL (jimmie-jams, MIT) is a BepInEx C# plugin plus Python socket server running PPO (Stable-Baselines3); it has beaten Lace 1. Silksong_AI (MattiaPasti) is vision-only: mss screen capture, OpenCV, and pydirectinput for input.
[mod19] (high) Several HK1 community tools have Silksong ports: DebugMod, Benchwarp, ItemChanger (work in progress), HKMP as SSMP, MapWarp, QuickWarp, the Architect level editor, the autosplitter and HKSplitMaker. HK1's Modding API, Satchel and a TasInfo equivalent do not.
[mod20] (high) Recommended decompilation and inspection workflow: open 'Hollow Knight Silksong_Data/Managed/Assembly-CSharp.dll' in ILSpy or dnSpyEx; use UnityExplorer (Cinematic Unity Explorer) or FsmMaster at runtime; use AssetRipper, AssetsTools.NET or rabex for offline asset and scene extraction.
-- implications:

===== speedrun (18 findings)
[spe0] (high,CRIT) The time-limited achievements are 'Speedrunner' (finish in under 5 hours) and 'Speed Completion' (100% completion and finish in under 30 hours). Steel Soul is 'Finish the game in Steel Soul mode'. Steel Heart is 100% plus finishing in Steel Soul.
[spe1] (medium,CRIT) The Speedrunner and Speed Completion limits use the save file's in-game timer, not real time. Pause and inventory stop it.
